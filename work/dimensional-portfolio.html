Personal project · 2026 · In progress

A portfolio in three dimensions.

The same person. The same work. Different ways to explore it.

Explore 1D · Open the pixel world ↗

The idea

I wanted a portfolio that could be both quiet and playful. On arrival, a visitor could choose the experience they feel like exploring: a page of essentials, a timeline, a pixel world.

The dimension names are creative labels for those experiences. Each world will carry the same core information about me and my work.

The three worlds

1D — The essentials · Available
Text, photographs, and links. A complete page you can read, navigate, print, and use even with JavaScript disabled.
2D — Along a line · Available
A guided path through the introduction, work, ideas, and contact details. Follow the line →
3D — The pixel desktop · Available
A little operating-system-inspired world with a personal pixel character and apps for work, notes, and contact. Open VivekOS ↗

What exists today

The 1D site is the starting point: a real portrait, a short introduction, project information, notes, and direct ways to contact me. It uses standalone pages with native navigation.

The guided line connects the introduction, work and ideas. The pixel desktop opens movable app windows and a neighbourhood arcade with seven games.

Built with

The 1D pages use HTML and a small stylesheet. The guided line and pixel desktop use JavaScript and Canvas. Vite builds all three worlds from this project.
