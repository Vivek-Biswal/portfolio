Personal project · 2026 · In progress

A portfolio in four dimensions.

The same person. The same work. Different ways to explore it.

Explore 0D · Open the 3D experiment ↗

The idea

I wanted a portfolio that could be both quiet and playful. On arrival, a visitor could choose the experience they feel like exploring: a page of essentials, a timeline, a pixel world, or a 3D character.

The dimension names are creative labels for those experiences. Each world will carry the same core information about me and my work.

The four worlds

0D — The essentials · Available
Text, photographs, and links. A complete page you can read, navigate, print, and use even with JavaScript disabled.
1D — Along a line · Available
A guided path through the introduction, work, ideas, and contact details. Follow the line →
2D — The pixel desktop · Available
A little operating-system-inspired world with a personal pixel character and apps for work, notes, and contact. Open VivekOS ↗
3D — The character · Experimental
A personalized character with pointer tracking, blinking, and reactions, set inside an interactive portfolio.

What exists today

The 0D site is the starting point: a real portrait, a short introduction, project information, notes, and direct ways to contact me. It uses standalone pages with native navigation.

The 3D prototype explores a customized rigged avatar and scroll-driven camera movement. Its design and likeness are still being refined; the experiment is currently on hold while I build the other worlds.

Built with

The 0D pages use HTML and a small stylesheet. The 3D prototype uses React, TypeScript, Three.js, and GSAP. Vite builds both versions from this project.

The 3D learning prototype began with MoncyDev’s portfolio repository. The original author’s credits and usage terms are retained in that part of the project.
