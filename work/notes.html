NOTE 01 / 03

One portfolio. A few ways in.

Why let visitors choose their world?

Some visitors want the essentials. Others want to explore. Each mode is another way into the same portfolio, and you can choose one directly.

1D is a direct page of text and images. 2D follows a guided line. 3D opens a pixel desktop and a little room to play.

NOTE 02 / 03

Keep the work easy to find.

A little restraint makes room for the story.

A playful interface still needs clear answers: what is the work, what was the idea, and how can someone get in touch?

The simple version gives that content a home. The other worlds add movement and exploration while keeping the important information easy to find.

NOTE 03 / 03

A character that feels personal.

Exploring personality, movement, and small reactions.

My photographs are the reference for the pixel companion. A smile, a wave, or a small reaction can add personality while leaving the work readable.

Click the arcade building, walk between cabinets, and choose a game. The projects and contact details stay easy to reach through the app windows.
