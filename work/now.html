What I'm working on.

I'm shaping a personal portfolio that can be explored in different ways, starting with the simplest version.

Making the basics feel complete

The current focus is the portfolio's foundation: text, photographs, useful links, and a comfortable reading experience. The 1D essentials now sit alongside 2D, a guided line through the work and ideas. I want someone to be able to find the work, understand the idea, and contact me without learning a new interface.

The first project here is the portfolio itself. Its project page explains how the different versions fit together.

A little room to play

The 3D pixel world brings together movable portfolio windows, a personal pixel companion, and seven games in Vivek Arcade.

Click the building to visit the arcade, walk between cabinets, and choose a game.

Ideas for later

The 3D pixel desktop now opens a little world of movable windows, a personal pixel character, and apps for work, notes, and contact. The next step is to add more projects and refine the experience as the portfolio grows.

Whichever version someone chooses, it should lead to the same work and the same way to reach me. The interface can change; the content should remain useful.
