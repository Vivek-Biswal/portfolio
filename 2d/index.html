More about me +

I’m Vivek Biswal. I enjoy the whole journey from “what if?” to a working experience: shaping an idea, finding a clear way to present it, and refining the details.

My current experiment is this portfolio. It explores how the same person and their work can be presented through different worlds, from a quiet page of words and photographs to a playful pixel desktop.

I’m drawn to useful interfaces, small thoughtful interactions, and projects that give me something new to learn.
