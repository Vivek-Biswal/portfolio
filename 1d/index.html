Curious by nature.
Careful by choice.

Between the linesBack to the essentials+

I’m Vivek Biswal. I enjoy the whole journey from “what if?” to a working experience: shaping an idea, finding a clear way to present it, and refining the details.

My current experiment is this portfolio. It explores how the same person and their work can be presented through different worlds, from a quiet page of words and photographs to a playful pixel desktop.

I’m drawn to useful interfaces, small thoughtful interactions, and projects that give me something new to learn.

03 /Loose thoughts.

  1. 01One portfolio. A few ways in.Why let visitors choose their world?Read noteClose note+

    Some visitors want the essentials. Others want to explore. Each mode is another way into the same portfolio, and you can choose one directly.

    1D is a direct page of text and images. 2D follows a guided line. 3D opens a pixel desktop and a little room to play.

    —
  2. 02Keep the work easy to find.A little restraint makes room for the story.Read noteClose note+

    A playful interface still needs clear answers: what is the work, what was the idea, and how can someone get in touch?

    The simple version gives that content a home. The other worlds add movement and exploration while keeping the important information easy to find.

    —
  3. 03A character that feels personal.Exploring personality, movement, and small reactions.Read noteClose note+

    My photographs are the reference for the pixel companion. A smile, a wave, or a small reaction can add personality while leaving the work readable.

    Click the arcade building, walk between cabinets, and choose a game. The projects and contact details stay easy to reach through the app windows.

    —
Still on my deskA look at what’s nextClose desk notes+

What I’m working on.

I’m shaping a personal portfolio that can be explored in different ways, starting with the simplest version.

Making the basics feel complete

The current focus is the portfolio’s foundation: text, photographs, useful links, and a comfortable reading experience.

Whichever world someone chooses, it should lead to the same work and the same way to reach me.

A little room to play

The pixel world brings together movable portfolio windows, a personal companion, and seven games in Vivek Arcade.

The next step is to add more projects and refine the experience as the portfolio grows.
